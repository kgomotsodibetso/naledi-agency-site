# **App Name**: Naledi Digital

## Core Features:

- Homepage Hero Section: Create a compelling hero section with a clear value proposition and a prominent 'Book a Strategy Call' CTA.
- About Section: Develop an 'About Us' section highlighting Naledi Digital's mission, values (strategy, creativity, precision), and a brief founder story.
- Services Page: Design a dedicated 'Services' page, with each service (Social Media Strategy, Website Building, etc.) presented in its own section detailing benefits, processes, and a relevant CTA.
- Portfolio/Case Studies Layout: Implement placeholder layouts for future portfolio items and client success stories to showcase Naledi Digital's expertise.
- Lead Capture Forms: Create functional 'Book a Strategy Call' and 'Submit Enquiry' forms with lead filtering capabilities, ensuring high-quality leads for the consultancy.
- Future-Proofing Section: Build a modular section designed to accommodate future digital products such as courses, templates, and community memberships.
- Client Persona Generation: AI tool that suggests marketing strategies tailored to specific client personas based on provided data and industry trends, leveraging the provided details and insights from the company's service offerings.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep navy blue (#192A56) to convey professionalism and sophistication, drawing from the need for dark neutrals. The saturation is appropriate to this choice, which is on the dark end of the spectrum.
- Background color: Very dark gray (#222222). It is closely related to the hue of the navy blue, but brought almost to black to support maximum readability. (In an RGB approximation, this would be #1A2955.)
- Accent color: Warm gold (#FFD700) as a subtle highlight, reflecting the 'star' concept. Its greater brightness and saturation allow it to stand out, while being relatively close in hue to navy blue, thus an analogous combination.
- Headline font: 'Poppins', a bold sans-serif for headers to convey a contemporary, precise feel.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', an elegant subtext which matches well with the other, and is very legible in extended passages.
- Minimalist, custom icons relevant to digital marketing services. Aim for a line-art style in gold to match the accent color.
- Clean and modern layout with generous spacing, ensuring a professional and uncluttered user experience. Maintain clear sections for easy navigation.
